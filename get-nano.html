Get Nano

Get Nano. Keep the keys.

The grove runs on Nano because it's feeless and instant. Both work better with a wallet of your own. Three steps, about ten minutes — most of it the ID check.

Step 01
Get a wallet.
Nault runs in a desktop browser. Natrium runs on your phone. Both are open source, and neither keeps anything on a server — the wallet is yours, not an account.
The one thing to know
Your wallet starts with a seed — 64 characters that are the money. Write it on paper. Nobody can reset it, not the wallet and not us.
